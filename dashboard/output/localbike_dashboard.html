What stands out

  • Baldwin Bikes brings in 71% of revenue. The two other stores have a similar basket size, they just get far fewer orders.
  • Discounts don't seem to move volume: a line sold at 20% off has about the same number of units as one at 5%. That is $775k given away over the period with no visible effect on quantity.
  • Road bikes have the largest stock value of all categories while ranking second in revenue. Together with the never-sold products above, there is a lot of cash sitting in the stores.
  • Roughly one order in three ships after the date promised to the customer, in all three stores.
  • 2018 looks like a collapse on the chart, but it is a data cut-off: orders from April 2018 are still pending or processing ($818k), not lost sales.
